> **Playbook 2 · v1 — drafted from Contentathon #3 (1 July 2026).** Grounded in real objections from PIs, senior postdocs and the RDM professionals who support them. To be tested and refined at the October rollouts.

**Who you're advocating to.** Driving the science, chronically short on time, thinks about FAIR mainly when a funder or journal makes them. Honest inner voice: *"Everyone around me seems to be winging it."*

## What actually stops them

The barriers they voted most important:

- *"I only engage when a funder or journal forces me."* — **the room's #1**
- *"I / my group don't have time."*
- *"I need dedicated staff with niche expertise — and not on fixed-term contracts, so there's legacy."*
- *"Who will pay for the extra time?"*
- *"I'll lose control of my data, or get scooped."*
- *"I'm not sure where to start — so many resources and opinions."*

Themes underneath: **time, funds, and expert hands-on support with continuity.** And a hard truth the room surfaced: *some people simply won't share — don't burn energy converting them; enable the willing.*

## What lands (the hooks that answer them)

The room voted **recognition** the single biggest lever, then **resource/expertise**, then **better processes**:

- **Recognition.** Credit FAIR the way we credit papers — CV metrics, badges, promotion criteria, awards ("champion your champions"). *"Metadata is a love note to the future."*
- **Saved time — framed as *their* time.** *How might we make a time-poor PI see FAIR as saving them time?* Data-management software that links the plan to the data *throughout* the grant, not a panic at the end.
- **Expert support, not more work for them.** "FAIR gurus" and shared research facilities costed into the grant at the writing stage — researchers can't do everything; an expert does it better and faster.
- **A bigger frame.** From a Galaxy talk the room loved: FAIR isn't a task, it's *"an organisational transformation — a way of thinking and working, like the scientific method."*

## How to deliver it

Recognition-led framings work as **a single strong slide + a story** (a data-reuse success from your own institute). The "no time" angle pairs well with a quick **demo** — "here's a stack of lab books, find one specific result" (see the [decision-makers module](../p2-05-decision-makers/); it lands for PIs too).

## What they actually say

> *"Now funders are asking me to fill in a box I can fill with AI, then go back to doing 'real' research."*
> *"FAIR only comes onto my radar at publication."*
> *"I need dedicated staff with the niche expertise — not fixed-term."*

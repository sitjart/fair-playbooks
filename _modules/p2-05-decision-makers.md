> **Playbook 2 · v1 — drafted from Contentathon #3 (1 July 2026).** Grounded in real objections from RDM leads, librarians and research-office decision-makers. To be tested and refined at the October rollouts.

**Who you're advocating to.** Holds budget, policy or staff time; under funder pressure but wary of who pays for the upkeep. Sees themselves as *"the bridge that frees up researchers' time,"* not the enforcer.

## What actually stops them

The barriers they voted most important:

- *"How do you assess a dataset's value, to prioritise where to invest limited resources?"* — **the room's clear #1**
- *"Fragmentation of support across library / data steward / local teams — whose budget?"*
- *"Researchers say they've no time — do I push or not?"*
- *"We'd need 100% FAIR compliance"* — versus understanding FAIR is a **journey**.
- *"What are the hidden costs — supporting secondary users, lost commercial opportunity?"*
- *"No consequence to non-compliance."* · *"I need evidence / ROI before committing staff or money."*

## What lands (the hooks that answer them)

**On prioritisation (how do we value data?):**

- *"FAIR data raises others' confidence in the research → more citation and reach."*
- *"Metadata is a love note to the future."* · *"Making data FAIR is like giving archaeologists a map — spend less time digging, more time discovering."*
- Turn the barrier into the session: **run an activity that defines all the ways a research object can have value.**

**On "researchers have no time":**

- *"There's no future value in data that isn't understandable — even to the team who made it."* Frame FAIR as **credibility insurance** in the age of AI slop.
- Get **expert support in early** (grant-writing stage), and **train PIs on DM software** that links plan → data across the whole grant.

**For the one exec slide:** cost of *not* making data FAIR · **FOMO** ("competitor institute X is already doing A, B, C") · funder/compliance risk · responsible-AI readiness · data as an exploitable asset · get "data risk" onto the **institutional risk register** so someone senior *owns* it.

## How to deliver it

Two demos the room rated highest:

- **The lab-books demo.** Hand people a stack of lab books; ask them to find one specific result — then estimate the research £ those notebooks represent. Visceral.
- **Lego Metadata for Reproducibility** — one team builds with instructions/metadata, one without; who finishes? ([doi.org/10.36399/gla.pubs.196477](https://doi.org/10.36399/gla.pubs.196477))

## What they actually say

> *"Perfect is the enemy of good — we don't want 100% FAIRness straight away."*
> *"Data re-use isn't considered novel research by funders, so why put effort in?"*
> *"UK-wide consistent approaches — I'm looking at you, BioFAIR."*

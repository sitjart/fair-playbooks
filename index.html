03 Make it yours

This is the difference between a kit and a course: you fill in your repositories, policies, contacts and examples once — and every activity starts speaking for your institution. No fork, so you never fall behind the kit.

1
Fill one short profile

On the set-up page: your facilitators, repositories, DMP and ethics contacts, local policies.

2
It flows everywhere

Every "ask your local RDM team" callout and slide placeholder now names your team — across the whole kit.

3
Always current

When the Club ships a new activity, you get it instantly — already wired to your profile. Nothing to re-sync.

Prefer to fork it? You can.

Forking is fine if your institution wants its own copy to change freely. But most teams don't need to — and there's a cost: a fork freezes the day you make it, so every new module and fix upstream passes you by, and you take on maintaining a diverging copy. The set-up page avoids all that — your details ride on top of one always-current kit, and new activities appear automatically. Fork when you genuinely need deep local changes; use the set-up page for everything else.
